About Me

Academic Background

I am a PhD student in the Human Nutrition Unit (Department of Food and Drug) at the University of Parma (supervised by Prof. Pedro Mena). My doctoral research focuses on the integration of heterogeneous multi-omics datasets (such as genomics, metabolomics, and gut microbiota) to explore inter-individual variability in dietary (poly)phenol processing and its relationship with cardiometabolic health.

I graduated from the University of Parma with a B.Sc. in Biotechnology and an M.Sc. in Genomic, Molecular and Industrial Biotechnology. During my university studies, I had the opportunity to carry out training internships:

Wageningen University & Research (Netherlands): Traineeships focused on colonic models (fecal batch cultures and SHIME dynamic systems) and an introduction to human apical-out colon organoids.

University of Parma: Laboratory traineeship in targeted metabolomics using HPLC-QQQ-MS/MS for the analysis and quantification of flavonoids in urine samples.

Chiesi Farmaceutici S.p.A.: Work experience as an analytical technician supporting dry powder inhalation product development.

Currently, in my PhD project, I work mainly with R and RStudio, basic command-line Bash, and basic HPC usage for data processing and statistical analysis.
